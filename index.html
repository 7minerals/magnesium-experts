About Magnesium Experts

Magnesium Experts is a research-driven publication covering magnesium and its role in muscle function, sleep, stress response, and long-term health. Our coverage centers on the differences between magnesium forms, how absorption actually works, the limits of standard deficiency testing, and the physiological mechanisms behind common recommendations.

Each article is grounded in current research and reviewed for accuracy before publication. We do not repeat wellness claims without explaining the mechanism behind them, and where the evidence is thin or mixed, we say so.

Content focus areas: magnesium forms and bioavailability, transdermal and oral absorption, deficiency and testing limitations, muscle and nerve function, sleep and stress physiology, and practical supplementation guidance. Sources are drawn from peer-reviewed nutrition and clinical research.
